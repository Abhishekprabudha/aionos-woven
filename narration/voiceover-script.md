# Voiceover Script — AIONOS × Woven

**Target duration:** under 5 minutes  
**Delivery:** confident, cinematic and human; deliberate pauses after the phrases “one control plane,” “customer,” and “the bridge to Woven.”

The complete narration is stored scene-by-scene in `data/story.json`. This file can be handed directly to a voice artist or imported into a production narration workflow.

## Opening
At Woven, innovation moves through vehicles, cities, people and infrastructure. AIONOS turns that frontier into one governed, learning operating system.

## Agent ensemble
The ensemble begins with HR Ground Ops—an agent that coordinates workforce readiness, exceptions and decisions where physical operations actually happen.

Last Mile Best Practices studies every delivery handoff, identifies friction, and recommends the next operational action before delays become customer problems.

NX Hero reads live traffic conditions and helps control movement across a city that never stops learning.

The Procurement Control Tower watches demand, suppliers, contracts and risk—turning fragmented signals into faster, defensible sourcing decisions.

UniFleet supervises autonomous and human-operated fleets, balancing availability, safety, utilization and mission priorities in real time.

Warehouse Vision sees inventory, movement and anomalies continuously—converting cameras and events into precise operational intelligence.

Driver Dashcam transforms every journey into evidence: detecting risk, explaining events, and improving safety across the fleet.

## UniStack
Different agents, different data, different operating contexts—yet one control plane. UniStack registers each agent, maps its tools and permissions, and connects it to governed data products. Every signal carries lineage. Every decision has policy. Every exception has an owner. Operators can observe performance, approve high-impact actions, investigate failures and replay the evidence behind a recommendation. Data is standardized across vehicle, city, partner and enterprise sources. Models and prompts are versioned. Human approvals are inserted exactly where risk demands them. This is how AIONOS moves from a collection of impressive agents to an accountable agentic enterprise—where data is reliable enough for autonomy, and autonomy is accountable enough for Woven.

## UniScale
Then UniScale turns operational evidence into market intelligence. It listens to customer, partner, media and ecosystem signals; measures narrative momentum, share of voice and brand health; and recommends the right story, audience, channel and timing. Every claim is linked to proof, approval and attribution. Communications teams can see which innovation themes are gaining trust, which audiences need evidence, and which messages require escalation. Woven's innovations are no longer communicated as isolated experiments. They become a compounding narrative of outcomes—visible to partners, trusted by stakeholders and measurable by leadership.

## UniWeave
UniWeave brings the entire system back to the person. A self-driving journey is not only a vehicle event; it is a connected experience spanning intent, weather, traffic, preferences, services and trust. UniWeave understands the context, orchestrates the right agents and systems, explains what changed, and keeps the customer in control. It remembers preferences only with consent, routes requests to people when empathy or authority is required, and converts every interaction into evidence for improvement. The result is mobility that feels personal, proactive and seamless—even when the world around it changes.

## Annotation intelligence
Behind every capable vision agent is rigorous annotation. AIONOS turns complex video into structured events, grounded objects, behaviours and risk labels—the mathematical foundation for agents that can see, reason and improve. Quality controls, disagreement analysis and hard-example review ensure that the agent learns from evidence rather than assumption.

Audio annotation adds another dimension: intent, emotion, events, context and acoustic patterns. Human expertise and mathematical logic convert raw sound into reliable intelligence that agents can act upon. The same governed workflow manages label definitions, reviewer confidence, model feedback and continuous improvement.

## Closing
Finally, UniStack closes the loop. Agent management, data operations, annotation logic, governance and no-code orchestration converge in one interface. New models, applications and autonomous systems can be connected without losing control. Policies travel with the data. Evidence travels with the decision. Outcomes return as feedback for the next iteration. Teams can assemble workflows visually, change approval thresholds, connect new tools and monitor impact without rebuilding the operating layer. This is the bridge to Woven: AIONOS can interface with the most advanced mobility technologies while making them observable, governable, resilient and ready to scale.

Woven creates the future mobility testbed. AIONOS makes the innovation operable, trusted and repeatable. Many agents. One fabric. Business outcomes at scale.
